Privacy Policy

  • 1) Who We Are & Scope

    1. Arise Society operates community programs incl)uding YUVA SETU and Jalaun Community Library.
    2. This Privacy Policy applies to our websites, forms, newsletters, events and other services.
    3. The policy aligns with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act.

  • 2) Key Definitions

    1. Personal Data: Information relating to an identifiable individual.
    2. Data Principal: The individual whose personal data is being processed.
    3. Data Fiduciary: Arise Society which determines how personal data is processed.
    4. Data Processor: Third-party services processing data on our behalf.

  • 3) What Data We Collect

    1. Name, email, phone number and contact details provided in forms.
    2. Registration information related to library membership or training programs.
    3. Donation payment confirmations from payment gateways.
    4. Messages, feedback and communication with our team.
    5. Technical information such as IP address, browser type and website usage.

  • 4) Why We Use Your Data

    1. Managing program registrations and library services.
    2. Sending updates about events, workshops and programs.
    3. Issuing donation acknowledgements and receipts.
    4. Improving our programs and services.
    5. Ensuring compliance with legal requirements.

  • 5) Children & Minors

    1. Many of our programs support students under 18 years of age.
    2. Registrations for minors may require consent from parents or guardians.
    3. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without permission.

  • 6) How We Share Data

    1. With payment gateways for donation processing.
    2. With program partners for training or placements when required.
    3. With legal authorities where required by law.
    4. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

  • 7) Data Retention

    1. Personal data is retained only as long as necessary.
    2. Data may be kept for legal, accounting or reporting requirements.
    3. Some anonymized data may be retained for research or reporting.

  • 8) Cookies & Analytics

    1. Cookies may be used to improve website functionality.
    2. Cookies help remember user preferences and analyze traffic.
    3. Users can control cookies through browser settings.

  • 9) Security Measures

    1. We use technical and administrative safeguards to protect personal data.
    2. Access to sensitive information is restricted to authorized personnel.
    3. Secure hosting and encrypted connections are used where possible.

  • 10) Photography, Video & CCTV

    1. Photos and videos may be captured during events for documentation.
    2. Participants may request to opt out of media usage.
    3. CCTV may be used in certain centres for safety and security.

  • 11) Your Rights

    1. Request access to your personal data.
    2. Correct inaccurate information.
    3. Request deletion where legally permitted.
    4. Withdraw consent for certain communications.

  • 12) Communication Preferences

    1. Users may receive essential notifications related to services.
    2. Optional communications may be unsubscribed.
    3. Preferences can be updated by contacting us.
